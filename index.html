The Helsinki Tour

4 hours. The best of Helsinki, by water.

A guided sport-RIB route through the city's most iconic waterfront sights and the quiet beauty of the inner archipelago.

  1. 01
    Market Square & Old Town

    The classic Helsinki skyline — Cathedral, Presidential Palace, Old Market Hall — seen the way it was first seen: from the sea.

  2. 02
    Suomenlinna Sea Fortress

    The 18th-century UNESCO World Heritage fortress, spread across six interconnected islands.

  3. 03
    Vallisaari Island

    Once a closed military island, now a lush nature reserve with cliffs, old fortifications, and Baltic panoramas.

  4. 04
    Lonna Island

    A tiny former mine-storage island with a public sauna, a restaurant, and one of the best sunset views in Helsinki.

  5. 05
    The Open Archipelago

    Run out past the inner islands to where the city disappears and the Baltic opens up. This is what the RIB was built for.
